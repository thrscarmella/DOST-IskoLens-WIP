Encoding Tips

Verify the scholar's PSA birth certificate before encoding the date of birth.
Ensure the municipality matches the scholar's Certificate of Indigency.
Scholarship Cycle should match the DOST Davao Oriental official cycle designation.
All required fields (*) must be completed before saving.
Once saved, this record will be added to the centralized DOST-IskoLens scholar database. You can edit or update the record at any time from Scholar Records.
